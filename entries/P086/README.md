# Deciding D-stability is coNP-hard

The computational difficulty of D-stability, raised by Chen, Fan and Yu ([1995](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-6911%2894%2900036-U)), is addressed by an explicit integer-matrix reduction from PARTITION. For positive weights of total 2T, the construction produces a matrix of order m + 4 that is D-stable exactly when no subset has weight T. When an equal split exists, some positive diagonal scaling has an eigenvalue with strictly positive real part. A Pell recursion supplies the parameters, with a logarithmic search bound and an explicit bound on every matrix entry. These bounds give polynomial binary output length. Together with the classical NP-completeness of PARTITION, the reduction yields coNP-hardness of D-stability and D-semistability, and NP-hardness of strict D-instability, even for a four-dimensional core with attachments at one coordinate.
